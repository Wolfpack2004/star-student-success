What we cover

Six things I teach, and nothing I don't.

Pick one, or combine them. Most students start with a single goal and add a second once the first one clicks.

Math

Math to Algebra 1

Whole numbers, fractions, decimals, percents, ratios, then straight into equations, graphing, and word problems. We fill the gaps first — that's usually what's blocking you.

Reading & writing

English Mastery

Grammar that holds up under pressure, reading for main idea and evidence, and writing a clear paragraph you can defend on a test or in an email.

Exams

Test Preparedness

GED and HiSET, placement exams, and the habits that carry across all of them: timing, elimination, and staying calm when the clock is loud.

Spanish

Conversational Spanish

Speaking from day one. Practical vocabulary for work, travel, and family, built around the conversations you actually need to have.

ESL

English Language Skills

English for work, school forms, appointments, and everyday life. Pronunciation, listening, and the confidence to ask someone to repeat themselves.

Citizenship

U.S. Citizenship Coaching

The 100 civics questions, the reading and writing portions, and full interview rehearsals — so the real interview feels like one you've already done.
